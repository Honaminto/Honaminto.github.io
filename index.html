Research Focus

Areas of Inquiry

My work sits at the intersection of climate science, political economy, and public health, using high-frequency data and causal inference methods.

01

Climate & Conflict

Linking climate extremes and economic stressors to conflict dynamics in Central America, West Africa, and South Asia using distributed-lag panel fixed-effects models.

02

Health & Welfare

Examining food security, governance, and social cohesion through household-level survey data across Sub-Saharan Africa, including COVID-19 impacts.

03

Migration & Agriculture

Understanding how El Niño/La Niña cycles shape agricultural productivity, food prices, and migration flows in Central America's Dry Corridor.

04

Machine Learning for Policy

Applying NLP and ML methods to large-scale news corpora and geospatial data to generate high-frequency conflict and political event datasets.

Working Papers

Recent Research

Empirical work combining panel data, causal inference, and geospatial methods.

2024–25

Climate Extremes and Economic Stressors as Predictors of Conflict in Central America (2012–2024)

20-country monthly panel integrating ERA5, SPEI, MLEED, EM-DAT, and FAO food-price series. Distributed-lag panel fixed-effects models with ElasticNet regularization and a mediation framework decomposing climate-to-conflict pathways.

Working Paper
2024–25

Does Exposure to Climate Change Affect Attitudes? A Contingent Yes

Theory-driven framework linking extreme weather exposure to climate change attitudes. Multi-specification inference strategies across diverse populations and geographies.

Working Paper
2023–24

Socio-Economic Impact of COVID-19 on African Economies, Social Cohesion, and Governance

Cross-country study using food-security and governance indicators from Benin, Burkina Faso, and South Africa. Harmonized multi-wave survey evidence.

Working Paper
2023–24

The Economic Impact of COVID-19 on Food Security in Benin and South Africa

Household-level food security analysis using FCS, DDS, and rCSI indicators from large-scale survey data in Benin and South Africa.
