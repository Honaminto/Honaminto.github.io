Current Projects

All papers combine large-scale panel datasets, causal inference methods, and policy-relevant empirical designs.

2024–25

Climate Extremes and Economic Stressors as Predictors of Conflict in Central America (2012–2024)

Built a 20-country monthly panel linking climate shocks (ERA5, SPEI, EM-DAT), economic stress (FAO food-price series), and conflict events (MLEED, MLP, ACLED). Applied ElasticNet regularization for data-driven lag selection and a mediation framework to decompose pathways through which climate shocks influence protest and violence. With Erik Wibbels (UPenn).

Working Paper
2024–25

Machine Learning for Peace (MLP) — Event Data Infrastructure

Contributed to a project scraping and processing over 120 million news articles from local outlets across 60+ developing countries to generate high-frequency event data on political and environmental dynamics. Contribution focused on web scraping and count verification to ensure data integrity across sources. With Erik Wibbels (UPenn).

Project Contribution
2024–25

Climate Change, Agriculture, and Migration in Central America

Examines how El Niño and La Niña cycles affect agricultural productivity and food prices across the Dry Corridor, and how resulting climate shocks and environmentally motivated violence shape migration flows toward the U.S. border. Featured in Penn Today and Phys.org (Jul. 2025). Supported by the Goldsmith Sustainable Agriculture Fund and Kleinman Center for Energy Policy. With Erik Wibbels, Irina Marinov, Anna Cabre & Jeremy Springman (UPenn).

Project Contribution
2024–25

The Credibility Revolution in Political Science

Assisted with data collection and validation for a study on replication practices and open science norms, including coding replication file policies across 175 journals and helping validate analysis code. With Guy Grossman (UPenn) and collaborators.

2024–25

School- and Community-Based Intervention Against Sexual Violence in Liberia

Cluster-randomized evaluation across 150 school-community dyads (2,160 girls, 1,187 caregivers). Conducted high-frequency data quality checks (HFCs) for baseline and endline surveys; monitored safeguarding trigger variables ensuring 26 sensitive disclosures were escalated; identified instrument assignment errors across 8 schools corrected prior to endline. With Guy Grossman (UPenn) and IPA/WHI.

2024

Does Exposure to Climate Change Affect Attitudes? A Contingent Yes

Theory-driven framework linking extreme weather exposure to climate change attitudes. Proposed multi-specification inference strategies across diverse populations and geographies to test heterogeneous treatment effects.

Working Paper
2023–24

Socio-Economic Impact of COVID-19 on African Economies, Social Cohesion, and Governance

Cross-country study using food-security and governance indicators from Benin, Burkina Faso, and South Africa. Harmonized multi-wave survey evidence to assess differential policy responses and welfare outcomes.

Working Paper
2023–24

The Economic Impact of COVID-19 on Food Security in Benin and South Africa

Household-level food security analysis using FCS (Food Consumption Score), DDS (Dietary Diversity Score), and rCSI (reduced Coping Strategy Index) indicators from large-scale survey data across two African economies.
